## Evidence-backed and unresolved service information

### timeline: reg-kjsea:kjsea-window

The 2026 KJSEA/KPLEA registration window was 2 March to 31 March 2026; it is historical.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: kjsea-2026 (p. 1, introduction)

### requirement: reg-kjsea:kjsea-eligibility

KJSEA registration is not automatic. Requirements include the Grade 9 database entry, KPSEA performance or equivalent, and Grade 7 and 8 school-based assessment scores.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: kjsea-2026 (p. 1, section 1)

### step: reg-kjsea:kjsea-declaration

The KJSEA institutional declaration verifies candidate names, assessment numbers and subject entries, including special-needs and religious-education options. It is forwarded through the SCDE.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: kjsea-2026 (PDF p7, institutional declaration)

### requirement: reg-kjsea:kjsea-special-needs

The KJSEA special-needs form requires institutional and medical evidence identifying assistance; its notes specify recent detailed medical and EARC reports for listed categories and a full-size photograph for physical disability. Low-vision candidates also require a medical report.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: kjsea-2026 (PDF p8, special-needs form notes ii-iv)

### orientation: reg-kjsea:placement-purpose

KJSEA results are used to show achievement by subject and help place a learner in a senior-school pathway based on the learner's interest and demonstrated strength; the nine KJSEA subjects are not combined into one aggregate value or points total.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: kjsea-regulations-2026 (PDF p17 sections 13.2-13.3)

### orientation: reg-kjsea:pathway-strength

The KJSEA Regulations state that a statistical formula determines a learner's strength in STEM, Arts and Sports Science, and Social Sciences. The pathway-strength value ranges from 0 to 75, with a higher value indicating stronger potential in that pathway.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: kjsea-regulations-2026 (PDF p17 section 13.4)

### orientation: reg-kjsea:score-composition

The Regulations state that KJSEA scoring for progression combines the Grade 9 KJSEA summative assessment at 60%, Grades 7 and 8 School-Based Assessment at 20%, and KPSEA at 20%.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: kjsea-regulations-2026 (PDF p17 section 13.5)

### requirement: reg-kjsea:learner-report

Schools are expected to download, print and officially stamp each KJSEA Individual Learner Report as guided by KNEC; learners progressing to Senior School are expected to have this report.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: kjsea-regulations-2026 (PDF p17 section 13.7)

### orientation: reg-kjsea:placement-factors-2025

For the pioneer 2025 placement, the Ministry of Education reported an automated process considering learner choices, performance, merit, equity, fairness and available school spaces. Learners had made 12 preferred senior-school choices across C1-C4 while selecting a pathway and three-subject combination with parent and teacher guidance.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: moe-kjsea-placement-2025 (PDF pp1-2 paragraphs 3 and 6)

### exception: reg-kjsea:placement-review-2025

The Ministry's seven-day placement-review window beginning 23 December 2025 was specific to the pioneer 2025 placement. It directed candidates to their Grade 9 school or Ministry Sub-County or County offices through heads of institutions; it must not be presented as a current or recurring window.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: moe-kjsea-placement-2025 (PDF p2 paragraph 8)
