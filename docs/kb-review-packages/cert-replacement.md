## Evidence-backed and unresolved service information

### orientation: cert-replacement:purpose

For applicants who sat KNEC examinations and lost or damaged certificates; applications use QMIS.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p1 introductory paragraph and section 1.0)

### requirement: cert-replacement:documents

Provide a certificate/result-slip copy (primary results before 2013 excluded), police abstract, identity evidence, Registrar of Persons confirmation and one colour photograph. Minors require birth-certificate and parent/guardian ID evidence. Upload copies.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p1 sections 2.1-2.6)

### requirement: cert-replacement:modules

Post-school modular applicants must attach results slips for all modules/parts.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p1 section 5.3)

### exception: cert-replacement:name-evidence

Identity names must match examination names; legal changes need supporting legal documents. Sworn affidavits are not accepted for that name-change evidence.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p2 section 5.5)

### step: cert-replacement:payment

Follow the system-issued M-Pesa payment details after document verification.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p1 section 3.2)

### step: cert-replacement:collection

Collect in person with original ID/passport; minors attend with a parent/guardian and the specified originals.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p1 section 4.1)

### fee: cert-replacement:fee

The charter lists KES 5,220 per certification letter.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: charter-2026 (p5 row 16)

### timeline: cert-replacement:timeline-conflict

Processing-time conflict: the guideline says 15 working days after requirements are fulfilled; the charter says 10 after complete, accurate documentation.

- Evidence status: `unresolved`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p1 section 5.2); charter-2026 (p5 row16)

### exception: cert-replacement:uncollected

The guideline says letters uncollected two years after application are disposed of without further notice or refund.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p1 section 4.2)

### exception: cert-replacement:non-replaceable

A certification letter cannot itself be replaced once issued.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p1 section 5.1)

### requirement: cert-replacement:loss-proof

Applicants must establish that the certificate was lost or destroyed.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p2 section 5.4)

### exception: cert-replacement:incomplete-application

Applications with inconsistent examination details or unmet requirements are declined.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p2 section 5.6)

### step: cert-replacement:online-only

Apply online; manual and emailed applications are not accepted. Visit for collection after the SMS notification.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p2 sections 6.0-7.0)

### contact: cert-replacement:archives-contact

The guideline lists archives@knec.ac.ke and ceo@knec.ac.ke for further enquiries.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: certification-2024 (p2 section 8.0)

### step: cert-replacement:portal-sequence

The public poster directs users from the KNEC website to QMIS, then registration/login, service details and attachments, submission for verification, and status checking for payment.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: qmis-procedure-poster (Poster steps 1-6, respective service column)
