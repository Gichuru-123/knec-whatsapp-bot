## Evidence-backed and unresolved service information

### orientation: ver-results:purpose

Confirmation of Results is an official statement used by an employer or institution to verify KNEC examination results. It is not a certificate, certificate replacement, examination-results correction or foreign-qualification equation.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p1 Background and p2 sections6.1-6.2); qmis-manual-2018 (PDF p6 sections2.4(vii)-2.4(ix))

### requirement: ver-results:eligible-requesters

An institution may submit employees' or students' certificates or result slips confidentially for verification, while a person may also apply individually through QMIS for a statement addressed to the receiving institution.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p1 section2.0)

### step: ver-results:apply

Apply through the Query Management Information System. Supply examination identity, school/centre, certificate evidence and the receiving institution address; business/technical requests also need the series.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p1 sections 1.0-1.8)

### step: ver-results:historical-qmis-sequence

The September 2017 guide shows selecting Confirmation, entering examination identity directly in QMIS, selecting examination, year and series where applicable, using Find, confirming the generated candidate name, entering recipient details, attaching each item requested by the Required List and submitting.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: qmis-external-2017 (PDF pp8-10, sections3.4.1-3.4.9)

### exception: ver-results:missing-copy

If certificate/results-slip evidence is missing, obtain a certified results printout through the appropriate former institution or education office. Primary results-slip year restrictions apply.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p1 sections 1.4-1.5)

### exception: ver-results:recipient

The statement is for the employer/institution, not the candidate or family; it cannot be redirected and is not a certificate.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p2 sections 6.1-6.2)

### step: ver-results:payment-prompt

The QMIS guidance says payment instructions are issued after document verification and the public procedure poster tells the applicant to submit, wait for verification and check Status for payment. Users should rely on the current portal prompt rather than payment details sent through chat.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: qmis-manual-2018 (PDF p5, post-submission payment message); qmis-procedure-poster (Confirmation steps4-6)

### fee: ver-results:fees

The 2026 charter lists KES 2,320 per certificate for confirmation within Kenya and KES 3,480 outside Kenya.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: charter-2026 (p4 row15(a)-(b))

### fee: ver-results:outside-email-courier

The 2026 charter additionally lists KES 1,392 for sending confirmation outside Kenya by email and says courier use is optional with extra charges met by the client.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: charter-2026 (p4 row15(c)-(d))

### fee: ver-results:modular-charging

The guideline charges modular courses per certificate and per module; do not treat one displayed tariff as the total for all modules.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p1 section 3.0)

### fee: ver-results:single-group-charging

Single and Group certificates are charged per stage when all or part of that stage is submitted together in one application.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p2 section 7.0)

### timeline: ver-results:processing-clock

The 2026 charter commits to 10 working days from receipt of complete, accurate documentation. The 2024 guideline says processing and dispatch take 10 working days but does not state the same starting event; do not promise an exact completion date.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: charter-2026 (p4 row15 timeline); confirmation-2024 (p2 section8.1)

### exception: ver-results:historical-results

The guideline warns that examinations from 1926 to 1984 may require longer processing.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p2 section 8.1)

### exception: ver-results:delivery-conflict

The 2024 guideline describes registered mail, optional courier and fax tariffs, while the 2026 charter describes within-Kenya and outside-Kenya sending, outside-Kenya email and optional courier. Current delivery and payment instructions must be confirmed before presenting a complete quote.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p1 sections3.0-5.0 and p2 section8.2); charter-2026 (p4 row15(a)-(d))

### requirement: ver-results:separate-examinations

Submit each examination in its own application with its certificate, result slip or certified results printout.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p1 section 1.8)

### requirement: ver-results:applicant-contact

Provide the applicant contact address and mobile number for clarification.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p2 section 8.3)

### contact: ver-results:archives-contact

The guideline lists archives@knec.ac.ke and ceo@knec.ac.ke for further enquiries.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: confirmation-2024 (p2 section 9.0)

### step: ver-results:portal-sequence

The public poster directs users from the KNEC website to QMIS, then registration/login, service details and attachments, submission for verification, and status checking for payment.

- Evidence status: `supported`
- Sources: qmis-procedure-poster (Poster steps 1-6, respective service column)
