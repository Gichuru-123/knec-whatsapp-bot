# Priority 2 evidence-gap register

These services remain research-only. The safe response is retained until authoritative, scoped evidence is inspected and approved.

## biodata-amendment

**Verified evidence currently available**

- biodata-amendment:service - The historical manual lists name, gender, entry code, birth-certificate number, ID number, birth year and citizenship amendments.
- biodata-amendment:official-query-route - The public query page directs examination queries through heads of institutions and Sub-County Directors of Education.
- biodata-amendment:historical-submission - The September 2017 manual describes selecting biodata amendment, entering examination identity, reviewing the existing details, entering the proposed correction, attaching the requested documents and submitting. This is historical workflow evidence, not confirmation of current eligibility or screens.
- The official query table defines multiple capture-error categories; the 2026 charter and captured query page provide conflicting deadline and penalty evidence that remains gated.

**Missing authoritative evidence and required official documents**

- Current eligibility by amendment type and examination.
- Current document checklist and QMIS screens.
- Exact deadline and penalty by examination, year and triggering event.

**Relevant or conflicting source records**

- queries-page
- charter-2026
- qmis-manual-2018
- qmis-procedure-poster

**Required scope**

- Exact service and query type.
- Examination and applicable year/series.
- Audience and triggering event.
- Effective source version.

**KNEC confirmation required**

- An accountable service owner must confirm the current procedure, applicable evidence, scope, fees, timelines, exceptions and controlling source version.
- Any contradiction must be resolved with an effective date and supersession record; absence of evidence must not be converted into a procedure.

**Safe current response:** Do not quote an unscoped deadline, fee or penalty. Clarify the service and examination; if the controlling rule remains unresolved, use controlled escalation to the verified KNEC switchboard.
