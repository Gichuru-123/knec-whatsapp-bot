## Questions for an authorized KNEC service reviewer

1. For certification, does the current service commitment use ten or fifteen working days, and precisely what starts the clock?
2. What is the current certification attachment checklist for adults, minors, modular qualifications and changed names? How does it reconcile with the poster's seven-document wording?
3. Does a pended certification application still require deletion and resubmission? What happens to previous attachments, references and payments?
4. Which biodata corrections may an applicant submit directly, and which require the school or education office? How are legal name changes distinguished from capture errors?
5. What documents, charges and deadlines apply to each biodata/photo/direct-recovery/results query, by examination and document type? Are the periods calendar or working days?
6. Which confirmation dispatch channels and overseas payment methods are currently supported, and how do module/stage charges apply?
7. For equation, what starts processing time, and are there later amendments to the 2 April 2025 translation, authentication and eligibility provisions? Confirm applicability for each proposed qualification.
8. Which published amendments supersede the downloaded 2026 registration circulars, and what is the intended treatment of late private KCSE entries?
9. Can KNEC provide an approved current public QMIS manual and dated screen guide, including status meanings and correction/resubmission behavior?

Record each answer with reviewer role, source or decision reference, effective date, service/examination scope and any superseded wording. These questions are prepared for review only; none has been sent.

## Equation evidence follow-up

A further reading of the already catalogued 2 April 2025 guideline closed nine missing-detail items: awarding-body conditions, Advanced Level evidence and threshold, KCSE subject condition, study duration, translation providers, direct authentication route, learner evidence, payment prompt and enquiry addresses. The question examples and equation plan now refer to these claims. This is completion of extraction from an existing source, not discovery of a newer policy.

The current search also surfaced older 2024 equation guidance and assessment-rule documents. Those were not substituted for the inspected 2025 procedure. An official domain alone does not establish that a rule document has been enacted or supersedes the operational guideline. No policy conflict has been resolved by assumption.

Eligibility remains a qualification-specific determination for KNEC. Processing-time start, present portal behavior and possible later amendments remain review questions. The other service journeys retain their existing review gates.
